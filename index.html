A procedural landscape

Forest to desert

One valley, five and a half kilometres of it, generated from noise and planted a species at a time. Fly south and the pines thin into woodland, the woodland dries into scrub, and the scrub opens out into dunes, cacti and an oasis. Nothing here is a photograph and nothing is hand-placed.
